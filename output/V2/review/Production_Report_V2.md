# CENTRIX V2 — production and validation

The revised 27-second commercial combines physical CGI objects, a continuous
golden data conductor and large Arabic typography. Attendance, collection and
follow-up transform into one unified attendance/accounts/reports system.
The AI section presents the supported absence-query example, followed by the
complete supplied logo and the Arabic brand line «سستم السنتر».

## Delivered media

- Master: 1080 × 1920, H.264 yuv420p, limited-range BT.709, 30 fps, 810 frames.
- Runtime: 27.000 seconds. Stereo AAC at 48 kHz.
- Review: 540 × 960, same 27-second cut with stereo audio.
- Central style test: 8 seconds with motion, narration, original music and SFX.
- Separate original narration MP3, music WAV, SFX WAV and final mix WAV.
- Editable React/TypeScript, Remotion and Three.js project with procedural
  textures, geometry, lighting, timeline, audio generation and render scripts.

## Review limitation

Physical phone-speaker listening is unavailable in the cloud environment.
A mono phone-band proxy was generated for technical review; listening on an
actual handset and subjective creative approval remain manual checks.
