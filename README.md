# CENTRIX — V2 cinematic commercial

The revised commercial uses physical CGI objects, a continuous golden data
conductor, large word-synchronized Arabic typography, and a new original
score and shot-specific effects. Attendance, accounts and reports converge
into one system, followed by the supported AI-query example and the supplied
brand mark. The original narration and complete logo asset are preserved.

The master is 1080×1920, 30 fps, 810 frames, exactly 27.000 seconds,
H.264 yuv420p / BT.709 with 48 kHz stereo AAC. The complete master decodes
without errors. Encoded loudness is −15.15 LUFS with −1.51 dBTP true peak.
Actual exported frames were inspected, including Arabic shaping, the unified
dashboard, illustrative AI answer and stable final brand hold.

The score, sound effects, CGI geometry, textures and reflection maps were
created procedurally without third-party samples. Product interfaces are
illustrative; no real student data or invented financial figures are used.
Physical handset listening remains a manual review, as documented in the report.
